# Known limitations

1. The one-quarter theorem is open. The active gap is Q-0019, not a completed
   terminal-absorption theorem.
2. F-0091's entropy count requires a faithful reconstructible execution log;
   its role is to force fork density, not to aggregate repeated actual edges.
3. F-0092 gives local/global multiplicity bounds inside a chosen two-coordinate
   replacement box. It does not prove that the one-coordinate heavy pair
   persists in future transitions.
4. F-0058 closes a future-complete fixed-anchor class only after persistence is
   established.
5. F-0078 does not remove partial-completion mass, construct a transition
   congruence, or turn a deterministic-policy return into an all-release core.
6. Fixed-instance finiteness and bounded computational searches do not provide
   uniform asymptotic constants.
7. The repository contains older route documents and evidence packages for
   auditability. Their historical claims do not override the current handoff,
   status card, main route, fact registry, or failure registry.
